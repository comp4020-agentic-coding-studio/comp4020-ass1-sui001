# Assignment 1 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was refusing to accept a plausible-looking page as "done."
The reaction-time test and timeline both passed the eye test early, but it was
turning the spec into failing tests before writing either feature that gave
me an actual target, and going back to replace guessed timescale numbers with
real cited research that turned a page that looked finished into one that
actually was. The check, not the glance, is what told me it was right.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It made me distrust my own first impression of "this looks done." A page that
renders is not the same claim as a page that's correct, and the gap between
those two only shows up if you build something — a test, a citation, a
check — that can catch it. I want to be the kind of developer who builds that
something before moving on, not after being caught out by it.
